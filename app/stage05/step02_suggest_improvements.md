# Step 2 – Ask Copilot to suggest UI improvements

**Goal:** Use Copilot as a design partner before writing any code.

## Prompt to give Copilot
> Working on the stage05 code, let's improve the application. Can you suggest some improvements to the user interface – perhaps a title?

## What to expect
Copilot replies with a ranked list and writes no code. Typical suggestions:
1. A title bar with a subtitle.
2. A legend explaining colour and size.
3. Filters (minimum rating, minimum number of ales) with a "showing X of Y" counter.
4. A search box.
5. Click for a pinned details popup, which also works on touch screens.
6. A pub list sidebar.
7. Polish: rating badge, "fit all" button, mobile layout.

## Say
"I get to choose what goes in. I'll take the title, legend, filters and click popup."
